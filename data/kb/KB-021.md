## Store credit only
The refund policy for sale items is different: sale and clearance items are refunded as Northwind store credit, not to your card or PayPal account.

## When the credit arrives
Store credit is added to your account within 2 business days of the return being inspected, and it never expires.

## Exceptions
Faulty sale items are refunded to the original payment method. Final Sale items are not refundable.
