title: How to File a Warranty Claim
category: warranty
---

## Step 1: gather details
You need the order number, photos of the defect, and a photo of the product label.

## Step 2: submit the claim
Open the warranty claim form in your account under Orders, upload the photos, and describe the problem. Claims are reviewed within 3 business days.

## Step 3: repair or replacement
If the claim is approved we send a prepaid label. Repairs take about 2 weeks; if a repair is not possible we replace the item.
