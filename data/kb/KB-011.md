## Return window
Footwear, including hiking boots and trail running shoes, can be returned within 30 days of delivery.

## Condition requirements
Footwear must be unworn outdoors. Try boots and shoes on indoors on a clean surface. Soles with dirt or visible wear cannot be accepted. Keep the original shoe box; it is part of the product.

## Wrong size
If the size is wrong, an exchange is usually faster than a return. See the exchanges article.
