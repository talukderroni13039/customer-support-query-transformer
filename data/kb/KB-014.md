## Return window
Tents, sleeping bags, stoves, and other camping equipment can be returned within 45 days of delivery.

## Condition requirements
Tents must be completely dry, clean, and packed in the original stuff sack with all poles and pegs. A tent that has been pitched outdoors on wet ground cannot be returned. Stoves must be unused.

## Missing parts
If a pole or peg is missing on arrival, contact support instead of returning the whole tent; we send spare parts free of charge.
