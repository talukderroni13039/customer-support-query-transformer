## Measure your torso length
Backpack size depends on torso length, not height. Measure from the bony bump at the base of your neck to the top of your hip bones.

## Choosing a size
Torso 40 to 45 cm is size S, 46 to 51 cm is size M, and 52 to 57 cm is size L. Ridgeline packs come in all three torso sizes.

## Adjusting the fit
Load the pack, tighten the hip belt first so it carries most of the weight, then the shoulder straps and load lifters.
