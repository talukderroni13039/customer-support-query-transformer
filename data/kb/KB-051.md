## How to measure
Stand on a sheet of paper in hiking socks, mark your heel and longest toe, and measure the distance in centimetres.

## Fit notes
Hiking boots run true to size. Trail running shoes run small, so order a half size up. Leave a thumb's width in front of the toes for descents.

## Width
Most models come in regular and wide. Wide adds 6 mm across the forefoot.
