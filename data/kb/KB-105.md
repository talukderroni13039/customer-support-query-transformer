## Overview
The Ridgeline 45 (RL-45) is a 45-litre backpack for weekend trips and hut-to-hut hiking.

## Specifications
Volume 45 litres, weight 1.3 kg. Dimensions 55 x 35 x 23 cm, so it is carry-on compatible with most airlines when not overpacked. Recommended load up to 14 kg. Includes a rain cover.

## Care instructions
Wipe the RL-45 with a damp cloth and mild soap. Do not machine wash; it damages the frame sheet and coatings.
