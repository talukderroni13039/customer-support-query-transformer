## Cards and wallets
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.

## Other ways to pay
You can also pay with Northwind gift cards, store credit, or in installments on eligible orders.

## Currency
Prices are charged in US dollars. Your bank may add a currency conversion fee for international cards.
