title: Changing or Cancelling an Order
category: orders
---

## Cancelling
An order can be cancelled within 1 hour of being placed from the Orders page. After that it is already being packed and cannot be stopped.

## Changing items
Items, sizes, and colors cannot be edited after checkout. Cancel within the hour and reorder, or exchange the item once it arrives.

## If it is too late
Refuse the parcel on delivery or start a return when it arrives; you are refunded in full.
