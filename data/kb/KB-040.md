## Standard warranty
Northwind Gear products are covered by a 2-year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship, counted from the delivery date.

## Longer warranties
Some product groups have longer or shorter cover: tents, backpacks, and footwear each have their own warranty article.

## What is not covered
Normal wear, accidental damage, improper care, and modifications are not covered.
