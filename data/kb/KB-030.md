## Delivery times
Standard shipping within the United States takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 2 business days.

## Costs
Standard shipping is free on orders over $75 and costs $6 otherwise. Express shipping costs $18.

## Alaska and Hawaii
Orders to Alaska and Hawaii take 5 to 8 business days. Express shipping is not available.
