## Overview
The Summit Shell (SS-110) is an uninsulated alpine hardshell for mountaineering and ski touring.

## Specifications
Waterproof rating 28,000 mm. Weight 480 g in size M. No insulation: it is designed to be worn over a mid layer. Two-way front zip, harness-compatible pockets, powder skirt.

## Care instructions
Machine wash the SS-110 at 30 degrees with technical wash and tumble dry low. Reproof once a season.
