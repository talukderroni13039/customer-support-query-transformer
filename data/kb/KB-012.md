## Return window
Outerwear, including jackets, shells, and insulated layers such as the Trailblazer Pro (TB-PRO-22) and Summit Shell (SS-110), can be returned within 60 days of delivery. The longer window lets you test the fit over layers at home.

## Condition requirements
Jackets must be unwashed and unworn outdoors, with all tags attached. Items that smell of smoke or detergent cannot be accepted.

## Seasonal purchases
Jackets bought in November or December can be returned until 31 January, whichever is later.
