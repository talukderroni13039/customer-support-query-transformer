title: Warranty for Tents
category: warranty

## Length of cover
Basecamp tents, including the Basecamp 2 (BC-200) and Basecamp 4 (BC-400), are covered by a 5-year warranty.

## What is covered
Seam failure, pole breakage under normal use, and zipper defects are covered. Tent poles are replaced free of charge for the life of the warranty.

## What is not covered
UV damage from leaving a tent pitched for weeks, mildew from storing it wet, and damage from storms above the rated wind speed.
