## Overview
The Cascade Hiker (CH-310) is a waterproof leather hiking boot for rough trails and heavy packs.

## Specifications
Full-grain leather upper with a waterproof membrane. Weight 620 g per boot in size 9. Stiff midsole, deep 5 mm lugs, resolable outsole.

## Care instructions
Brush dirt off the CH-310 after each hike, treat the leather with wax every few months, and dry at room temperature.
