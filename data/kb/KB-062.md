## How it works
Orders over $100 can be split into four interest-free payments, charged every two weeks.

## Eligibility
Installments are available to customers in the United States with a debit or credit card.

## Limits
Installment plans cannot be combined with gift cards or store credit in the same order, and cannot be used to buy gift cards.
