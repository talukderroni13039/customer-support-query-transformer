## Overview
The Ridgeline 65 (RL-65) is a 65-litre backpack for multi-day trekking with a full camping load.

## Specifications
Volume 65 litres, weight 2.1 kg. Dimensions 78 x 38 x 30 cm; it is too large for carry-on and must be checked in. Recommended load up to 23 kg. Adjustable torso length and a removable lid.

## Care instructions
Wipe the RL-65 with a damp cloth and mild soap. Do not machine wash. Store it dry with the straps loosened.
