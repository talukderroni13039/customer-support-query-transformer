## Length of cover
Ridgeline backpacks, including the Ridgeline 45 (RL-45) and Ridgeline 65 (RL-65), carry a lifetime warranty for the original owner.

## What is covered
Broken buckles, failed zippers, torn seams, and frame defects are repaired or replaced at no cost, however old the pack is.

## What is not covered
Airline damage, animal damage, and fabric worn through by abrasion.
