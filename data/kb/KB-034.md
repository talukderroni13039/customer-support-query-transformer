## Delivery times
Standard shipping to European Union countries takes 7 to 12 business days.

## Costs
Shipping to the EU costs a flat $22, and is free on orders over $200.

## Duties and taxes
VAT for the destination country is collected at checkout under the IOSS scheme for orders up to 150 EUR.
