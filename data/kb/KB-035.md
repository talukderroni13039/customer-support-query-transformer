## Tracking an order
A tracking link is emailed when the order leaves the warehouse. It can take 24 hours for the carrier to show the first scan.

## Delayed parcels
If tracking has not updated for 5 business days, contact support and we open a trace with the carrier.

## Lost parcels
A parcel is treated as lost 15 business days after the last scan. We then send a replacement or refund the order.
