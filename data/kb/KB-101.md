## Overview
The Trailblazer Pro (TB-PRO-22) is a fully waterproof three-layer shell jacket for hiking in sustained rain.

## Specifications
Waterproof rating 20,000 mm, breathability 20,000 g. Weight 410 g in size M. Fully taped seams, pit zips for ventilation, helmet-compatible hood, two hand pockets.

## Care instructions
The TB-PRO-22 is machine washable at 30 degrees on a gentle cycle with technical wash. Tumble dry low to reactivate the water-repellent finish. Do not use fabric softener.
