## How to measure
Measure your chest at the fullest point and your sleeve from the centre of the neck to the wrist.

## Fit notes
Shell jackets are cut with room for a mid layer. If you are between sizes and plan to wear a thick fleece underneath, choose the larger size. Insulated jackets have a closer fit.

## Size chart
S fits chest 91 to 97 cm, M 98 to 104 cm, L 105 to 112 cm, XL 113 to 120 cm.
