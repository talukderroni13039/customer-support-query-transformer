## Overview
The Trailblazer Lite (TB-PRO-23) is a lightweight water-resistant two-layer jacket for day hikes and showers. It is not fully waterproof.

## Specifications
Water resistance rating 8,000 mm, breathability 10,000 g. Weight 290 g in size M. Critically taped seams, no pit zips, adjustable hood, packs into its own chest pocket.

## Care instructions
The TB-PRO-23 is not machine washable: hand wash only in cool water with technical wash. Hang to dry and do not tumble dry, because heat damages the lightweight membrane.
