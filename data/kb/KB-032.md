## Delivery times
Standard shipping to the United Kingdom takes 7 to 12 business days.

## Costs
Shipping to the UK costs a flat $20, and is free on orders over $200.

## Duties and taxes
UK VAT is collected at checkout. Orders above 135 GBP may also be charged import duty by the carrier on delivery.
