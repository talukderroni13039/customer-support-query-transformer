## Earning points
Trail Rewards members earn 1 point per dollar spent. Joining is free from the account page.

## Redeeming points
Every 200 points become a $10 reward voucher, issued automatically.

## Member benefits
Members get free standard shipping on every order in the United States and early access to sales.
