category: returns

## Return window
Items bought at a reduced price in a sale or from the clearance section can be returned within 14 days of delivery. This is shorter than the standard window.

## Final Sale items
Products marked Final Sale on the product page and on your receipt cannot be returned or exchanged.

## How you are paid back
Returned sale items are paid back as store credit, not to the original payment method. See the article on refunds for sale items.
