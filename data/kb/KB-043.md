title: Warranty for Footwear
category: warranty
---

## Length of cover
Footwear, including Cascade boots and trail shoes, is covered by a 1-year warranty.

## What is covered
Sole separation, failed eyelets, and leaking waterproof membranes within the first year.

## What is not covered
Worn-out tread and damage from drying boots next to a heater or fire.
