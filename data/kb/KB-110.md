## Overview
The Cascade Trail Runner (CH-315) is a lightweight, non-waterproof trail running shoe for fast days on dry trails.

## Specifications
Breathable mesh upper without a membrane. Weight 280 g per shoe in size 9. 6 mm heel drop, 4 mm lugs, rock plate in the forefoot.

## Care instructions
Rinse the CH-315 with cold water and remove the insoles to air dry. Do not machine wash or tumble dry.
