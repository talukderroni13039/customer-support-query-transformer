## Step 1: open the returns portal
Go to Orders in your account, choose the order, and select Return items. Guests can use the order number and email address.

## Step 2: print the label
A prepaid return label is emailed to you. Return shipping is free within the United States; international customers pay a flat return fee that is deducted from the refund.

## Step 3: send the parcel
Drop the parcel at any carrier location within 7 days of creating the label. Keep the drop-off receipt until the refund arrives.
