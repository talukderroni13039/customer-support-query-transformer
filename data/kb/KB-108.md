## Overview
The Basecamp 4 (BC-400) is a four-person, three-season tent for family camping.

## Specifications
Sleeps 4. Packed weight 4.1 kg. Floor area 5.6 square metres, peak height 130 cm. Flysheet waterproof rating 3,000 mm, floor 5,000 mm. One large door and a covered porch.

## Care instructions
Always dry the BC-400 fully before storing it. Clean with a sponge and cold water only; never machine wash a tent.
