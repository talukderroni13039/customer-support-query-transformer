## Standard return window
Most Northwind Gear items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund. Some product groups have their own window: outerwear, footwear, tents and camping equipment, and sale items each have a dedicated policy article.

## General conditions
Items must be clean, unused, and in their original packaging with tags attached. Proof of purchase such as an order number or gift receipt is required.

## Items that cannot be returned
Gift cards, personalised items, and items marked Final Sale cannot be returned.
