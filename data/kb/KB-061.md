## Buying gift cards
Digital gift cards are available from $25 to $500 and are emailed to the recipient within minutes.

## Expiry
Gift cards and store credit never expire and have no fees.

## Using them
Enter the gift card code at checkout. Several gift cards can be combined in one order, and any remaining balance stays on the card.
