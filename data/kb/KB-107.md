## Overview
The Basecamp 2 (BC-200) is a two-person, three-season backpacking tent.

## Specifications
Sleeps 2. Packed weight 1.9 kg. Floor area 2.8 square metres, peak height 105 cm. Flysheet waterproof rating 3,000 mm, floor 5,000 mm. Two doors and two vestibules.

## Care instructions
Always dry the BC-200 fully before storing it. Clean with a sponge and cold water only; never machine wash a tent.
