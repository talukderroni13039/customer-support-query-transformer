## Returning a gift
If you received an item as a gift, you can return it with the gift receipt or the order number from the packing slip.

## Who gets the refund
Gift returns are refunded to the recipient as store credit, so the buyer is not notified. If the buyer returns the item themselves, the refund goes to their original payment method.
