## When the refund is issued
A refund is issued once the returned parcel is scanned and inspected at our warehouse, usually 2 to 3 business days after it arrives.

## How long until the money arrives
After the refund is issued, card payments take 5 to 7 business days to appear on your statement. PayPal refunds arrive within 24 hours. If the money has not shown up after 10 business days, contact your bank with the refund reference.

## Refund method
Refunds go back to the original payment method. Original shipping costs are not refunded unless the item was faulty.
