## How exchanges work
You can exchange an unused item for a different size or color within its return window. This covers clothing and footwear, including going a half size up or down in boots.

## Starting an exchange
Choose Exchange instead of Return in the returns portal and pick the new size or color. We ship the replacement as soon as the carrier scans your return parcel, so you do not wait for inspection.

## Price differences
If the new item costs more you pay the difference; if it costs less we refund the difference.
