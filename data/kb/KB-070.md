## Forgotten password
Select Forgot password on the sign-in page and enter your email address. A reset link is sent within a few minutes and is valid for 1 hour.

## Forgotten email address
If you no longer know which email you signed up with, contact support with a recent order number and we will find your account.

## Locked account
After five failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes.
