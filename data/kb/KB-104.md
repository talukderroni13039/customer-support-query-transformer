## Overview
The Summit Shell Insulated (SS-111) is the warmer version of the Summit Shell, with synthetic insulation for cold, wet winter days.

## Specifications
Waterproof rating 20,000 mm. 80 g synthetic insulation in the body and 60 g in the sleeves. Weight 720 g in size M. Comfort rated to minus 15 degrees Celsius when active.

## Care instructions
Machine wash the SS-111 at 30 degrees on a gentle cycle and tumble dry low with two dryer balls to restore loft.
