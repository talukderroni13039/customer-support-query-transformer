## What we repair
Northwind Gear repairs torn flysheets, broken tent poles, and stuck zippers on any Basecamp tent, including the BC-200 and BC-400, even after the warranty has ended.

## Prices
A pole section replacement costs 12 dollars. A flysheet patch costs 20 dollars. A full zipper replacement costs 45 dollars. Repairs covered by warranty are free.

## How long it takes
Send the tent clean and dry with the repair form. Repairs take about 10 business days plus return shipping.
